One loop, one number

Draw a closed loop in a flowing river and add up how much the water pushes along it. That total is circulation, and it is what a turbine blade turns into torque.

Line integral

Dot the velocity field with each tiny step along the loop. Positive means the flow runs with you.

Γ = ∮ F · dr

Green’s theorem

Skip the loop: add up the local curl inside it instead. Both routes give the same answer, which the lab checks live.

Γ = ∬ (∂Q/∂x − ∂P/∂y) dA

Euler’s turbine

Change in swirl across the runner is torque on the shaft. More useful swirl in, more electricity out.
